Price

No candlesticks, on purpose. A candle needs an open, high, low and close inside every interval. Here there are a handful of atomic swaps a day, so those four numbers would be derived from one or two trades: a shape that says "market" while carrying almost nothing. Price and volume are also two charts rather than one with two y-axes, which is the chart mistake that misleads most reliably.

Order book

Floor ...

Each card is one carrier, signed and ready, and it sells whole: a listing cannot name a partial amount, because the buyer builds the transfer instructions and could simply leave them out and take the lot. Selling the carrier entire removes that rather than patching it.

That does not mean buying in fixed lots. Pick several and they settle in one transaction, all or nothing, which is what the maker's signature type is for. For an exact quantity, make an offer instead: see below.

Holders are outputs carrying a balance, not addresses: the protocol never sees an address. One person may hold twenty carriers, so read this as concentration of coins, not of people.

BUY AN EXACT AMOUNT

Make an offer

Want 137 and not 500? Name the amount and the price. A seller fills it by splitting a carrier down to exactly that and signing the whole transaction themselves, so there is nothing left for anyone to rewrite. That is why an offer may name a partial amount when a listing may not: the side that builds the transaction is the side that is exposed, and here it is the seller.

So there are three ways to buy: take one carrier, sweep several in one transaction, or offer for an exact amount. Only the first is what a Runes marketplace gives you.
