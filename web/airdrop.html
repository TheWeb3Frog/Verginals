How the split works

Every action is worth exactly one share.

Not weighted by how much you spent, how early you were, or how many times you did it. One person who inscribed once and one person who inscribed four hundred times hold the same single share, and somebody who did all four holds four.

The billion is then divided by every share that exists, so your allocation depends on how many people qualify. That number is still moving, and the figure this page shows you is today's, not a promise.

What it costs you

Nothing, and there is nothing to claim.

No claim transaction, no approval, no fee. The coins arrive in the wallet that earned them, and a wallet that does nothing still receives them.

Where the list comes from

The chain, not our database.

Every action counted here left a transaction on Verge. The index is rebuilt from those blocks on every restart, so anyone running the same code arrives at the same list.

What we kept

None of it.

No founder wallet, no treasury, no marketing bag, no vesting cliff quietly unlocking in a year. The supply is fixed at a billion and the mint is closed for ever.
