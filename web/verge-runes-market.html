WHAT IS DIFFERENT HERE

Three things this market cannot copy

  • Listings sell a whole carrier, not an amount. A balance lives on an output, so a listing offers that output and everything on it. You cannot buy 137 of a lot of 500. The token page shows the split-then-list flow, and every listing card leads with everything the carrier holds, because selling a token and not noticing the inscription riding along is the expensive mistake.
  • The floor is a real floor. It is the cheapest carrier actually listed and signed, not an aggregate of asks that may not be honoured. Every listing here is a partially signed transaction: it either executes atomically or it does not exist.
  • Prices come from settled trades only. No mid-price from an order book, because there is no order book: there are signed offers and completed swaps. A market with four trades a day looks like four trades a day.
