What it is for

Nothing.
That is the whole idea.

no roadmap no utility no promises

Nothing is launching in Q3. There is no partnership to announce, no staking, no burn, no cliff with somebody's bag behind it. ALPHA BRRRR does not do anything, and it was never meant to.

Here is what it is instead. Verge has been running for a decade and until this year nobody had ever etched a coin on it. The addresses on this list inscribed the first things, minted the first Alphas and took the first tickers. That only happens once on a chain. It already happened, and these are the people it happened to.

So the coin is a receipt. 21,000,000 of them, split between everyone who was in the room, so that the room has something to hold in common. It is the first community coin Verginals has ever made and there was no clever reason to make it.

Trade it, sit on it, or forget it in a wallet for three years. All three are fine. It is yours, and we are not going to tell you what it means.

How the split works

Every action is worth exactly one share.

Not weighted by how much you spent, how early you were, or how many times you did it. One person who inscribed once and one person who inscribed four hundred times hold the same single share, and somebody who did all four holds four.

The whole supply is then divided by every share that exists, so your allocation depends on how many people qualify. That number is still moving, and the figure this page shows you is today's, not a promise.

What it costs you

Nothing, and there is nothing to claim.

No claim transaction, no approval, no fee. The coins arrive in the wallet that earned them, and a wallet that does nothing still receives them.

Where the list comes from

The chain, not our database.

Every action counted here left a transaction on Verge. The index is rebuilt from those blocks on every restart, so anyone running the same code arrives at the same list.

What we kept

None of it.

No founder wallet, no treasury, no marketing bag, no vesting cliff quietly unlocking in a year. The supply is fixed at 21,000,000 and the mint is closed for ever.
