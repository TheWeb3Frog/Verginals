    How a listing works

    A price, published. Not a deposit.

    Your coins stay in your wallet. Nothing moves until you accept an offer yourself, and a listing comes down whenever you say so.

    Where the balance comes from

    Proven against the chain, not our server.

    Your wallet checks what a seller's coins really hold against the merkle root published on chain before it signs. If that check fails, no offer is built.

    What we take

    Nothing. The only cost is the network's.

    No listing fee, no sale fee, no cut of a mint. The fee a claim pays goes to whoever mines the block.
