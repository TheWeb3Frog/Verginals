1

The name

Up to 26 letters, A to Z. Press space for a separator: it is display only and costs nothing, so DOG•GO and DOGGO are the same coin and lock the same amount.

What a wallet shows next to an amount, the way $ sits next to a price. Left empty it is ¤, the generic currency sign. One character, so it cannot be used to pretend to be somebody else. There is no free text name: your ticker is the name.

3

Who else can get some

Leave this off and the supply is only what you kept. Turn it on and anyone may claim a fixed amount, first come first served, until the supply runs out.

How many coins one claim hands over. Claiming runs until the supply is gone.

Zero is the recommendation. A claim then costs only what the network itself charges to relay it, which is the cheapest a mint can be and the single biggest reason people actually claim. Whatever you set is paid as an ordinary transaction fee, so the miner of the block receives it and the protocol pays nobody. Maximum 49 XVG, because Verge Core refuses a fee above 50.

What a price is for. It is the only friction on your mint. Nothing limits how many times one wallet may claim, so at zero one person can take your whole supply in an afternoon for the cost of relay. Most etchers should still choose zero and accept that, because a mint nobody claims is worth less than one somebody swept. Set a price only if you would rather have a slow mint than a fast one.
