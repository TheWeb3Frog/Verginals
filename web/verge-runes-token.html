Price

No candlesticks, on purpose. A candle needs an open, high, low and close inside every interval. Here there are a handful of atomic swaps a day, so those four numbers would be derived from one or two trades: a shape that says "market" while carrying almost nothing. Price and volume are also two charts rather than one with two y-axes, which is the chart mistake that misleads most reliably.

Order book

Floor —

Each card is one carrier, signed and ready. Buying takes it whole: there is no partial fill, because a balance lives on an output and half an output does not exist. To sell part of a holding you split the carrier first, in a separate transaction.

Holders are outputs carrying a balance, not addresses: the protocol never sees an address. One person may hold twenty carriers, so read this as concentration of coins, not of people.
