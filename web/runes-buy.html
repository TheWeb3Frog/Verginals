    What actually happens

    Your wallet builds the whole transaction and signs everything except the seller's own coin. The seller can accept it or ignore it, and they cannot change a single number in it. Nothing leaves your wallet until they sign.

    Your wallet checks what the seller's coins really hold against the root published on chain, not against anything this site says. If that check fails, no offer is built.
