Three things to decide, one price to pay, and none of it can be changed afterwards. Nothing is broadcast until you say so, and the total is shown before anything is signed. How the protocol works.

Checking what this server reports…

1

The name

Up to 26 characters, A to Z and 0 to 9. Press space for a separator: it is display only and costs nothing, so DOG•GO and DOGGO are the same coin at the same price.

What a wallet shows next to an amount, the way $ sits next to a price. One character, so it cannot be used to pretend to be somebody else. There is no free text name: your ticker is the name.

2

The supply

How many units will ever exist, and how many you keep for yourself. Neither can change afterwards.

0%

3

Who else can get some

Leave this off and the supply is only what you kept. Turn it on and anyone may claim a fixed amount, until the cap is reached.

This is paid as an ordinary transaction fee, so the miner of the block receives it and the protocol pays nobody. It is what stops one person taking the whole supply for the cost of relay. Maximum 49 XVG, because Verge Core refuses a fee above 50.

4

Where it goes, and the key that gets your money back

The ticker price is not spent. It sits in an output only you can reopen, four years from now. That key is generated here, in your browser, and never sent anywhere.
