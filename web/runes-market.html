    Nothing here is held for you

    A listing is a signed price, not a deposit. Your coins stay in your wallet until you accept an offer yourself, and you can take a listing down at any time. This site never holds a key and cannot move anything.

    Every balance is proven

    Your wallet checks what a seller's coins really hold against the merkle root published on chain before it signs anything, rather than believing this page. If that check fails, no offer is built.
